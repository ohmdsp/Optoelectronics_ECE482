Welcome to the ECE 482/582 Optical Electronics web site. This site contains information and downloadable materials for the laboratory experiments that you will be required to complete during the course. Under "Experiments" listed on the right of this page are links to the documentation for each required lab experiment. You should download the PDF file that corresponds to each experiment and complete the preparatory reading and questions before your assigned lab time. Keep in mind that your preparation for each experiment will be critical to your success in the lab.

This site also contains some additional resources that are referenced in the lab experiments. The "Resources" at the top of the page will take you to them.

Each student will complete an individual design project for the course. The design project description and required proposal document can be downloaded by clicking on the "Project" link above.
